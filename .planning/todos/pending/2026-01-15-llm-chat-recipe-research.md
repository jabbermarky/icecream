## Problem

Creating new ice cream recipes requires research - understanding flavor combinations, ingredient ratios, techniques for specific textures, etc. Currently this research happens outside the app (web searches, cookbooks, asking ChatGPT separately).

An integrated LLM chat interface within Ice Ed would allow users to:
- Ask questions about ice cream science while formulating
- Get suggestions for flavor pairings
- Troubleshoot texture issues
- Research ingredient substitutions
- Learn techniques (e.g., "how do I make a stretchy Turkish ice cream?")
